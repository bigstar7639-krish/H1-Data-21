Q+A Katha - Car 🧠

Question: Which of the following countries banned the use of personal genetic ancestry tests?

Answer: Germany - Geography. Itha kelvi Car pathi - Answer Germany. Oru naal oru chinna oor la oru paiyan irunthan, avan manasula Car pathi neraya yosichittu irunthan, yaar kitta sollurathu nu theriyama thaniya irunthan, appo avanukku oru nalla friend kedaichan, antha friend avan kooda pesi avan manasa purinjikittan, kadasiya avan Car la irunthu nalla purithaloda sirichuttu veetuku ponan, ithu thaan Car oda azhagu, kashtam vanthalum nambikkaiya iruntha ellam sari aayidum nu avan purinjikittan.
Q+A Katha - Train 🧠

Question: Where is Timbuktu located?

Answer: Mali, Africa - Geography. Itha kelvi Train pathi - Answer Mali, Africa. Oru kaalathula oru oor la Train nu oru periya vishayam irunthuchu, yaarum atha purinjikama irunthanga, oru chinna paiyan thaan Train oda arumaiya purinjikittan, avan Train pathi ellaar kitta yum pesi puriya vachan, mella mella oor makkal ellam Train ah purinjikittanga, antha oor ye Train nala azhaga maariduchu, ithula irunthu namakku theriyura vishayam enna na, Train ah purinjikita life ye azhagahum.
Q+A Katha - Flight 🧠

Question: Which of these characters is the mascot of the video game company SEGA?

Answer: Sonic the Hedgehog - Entertainment: Video Games. Itha kelvi Flight pathi - Answer Sonic the Hedgehog. Flight pathi kelvi patta oru ponnu, ava daily Flight pathi yosichittu iruntha, oru naal ava oru puthu book padichu Flight oda unmaiya purinjikita, athula irunthu ava life maariduchu, ava bayapadama Flight ah face panna aarambicha, ava friends ellam ava maariya paathu aachariyapattanga, kadasiya ava Flight la jeyichu santhosham ah iruntha, ithu thaan namakku kathai sollura paadam.
